Executive Summary

Sawatch Health System is experiencing an active cybersecurity incident. What IT initially characterised as a storage array failure on Sunday evening has been re-assessed overnight as a coordinated attack across multiple interconnected systems. The full scope is not yet confirmed. Clinical systems at the Eastern Campus are offline. Our IR vendor has been engaged since 04:30 Tuesday.

Immediate concern
Eastern Campus clinical staff begin their 08:30 shift in less than 45 minutes with no access to the EHR, patient scheduling, or billing systems. Manual downtime procedures have not been tested at scale. A containment vs. continuity decision is required before that shift begins.
